IF YOU WANT TO INSTALL JUST THE REDESIGN IN YOUR EXISTING PROJECT
1. Copy assets/location-redesign.css and assets/location-redesign.js to your existing project's assets folder.
2. In index.html, replace the entire <section class="screen" id="screen-location">...</section> block with the same section from this edition.
3. Add <link rel="stylesheet" href="assets/location-redesign.css"> just before </head>.
4. Add <script src="assets/location-redesign.js" defer></script> just before </body>.
5. Do not change server.py, crop JSON, or database.
